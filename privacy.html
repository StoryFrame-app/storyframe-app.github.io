Privacy policy

StoryFrame collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no servers. The app never connects to the internet.

Your photos stay on your iPhone. StoryFrame reads only the photos you choose, including their camera details, to draw the frame. It saves new images to your library only when you tap Save, and it never changes or deletes your originals. If Photos reports an error while saving a very large export, StoryFrame may ask to see your library, only to check whether that image arrived.

What's stored: your settings and presets, on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Metadata in exports: exported files contain no camera details, date or location unless you turn them on under Metadata forwarding when you export.

Purchases go through Apple's App Store, which handles the payment. StoryFrame only learns which plan you have; it never sees your Apple ID or payment details.

If this ever changes, the app will tell you before it happens, and this page will be updated.

Contact: support.storyframe@icloud.com. If you write to this address, your email is used only to answer you.
