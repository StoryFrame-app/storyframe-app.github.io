StoryFrame support

StoryFrame puts your photo on a clean backdrop with the camera details underneath, sized for Instagram Stories, posts and carousels.

Questions, a bug, or a photo that doesn't open? Write to support.storyframe@icloud.com. It helps to say which iPhone and iOS version you have and, for a bug, to attach the log from Settings › Lab › Log.

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.
